Language & Voice

AI Key

Voice AI uses a free open-source AI through Groq. Get a free key (no credit card) at console.groq.com/keys.

Gmail & Contacts

Lets the AI send email after you confirm by voice. Needs a one-time Gmail app password: turn on 2-Step Verification, then create one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.

Advanced

Hotkey: hold Left Ctrl + Left Alt to talk, let go to send.
