Gmail & Contacts

Let the AI read your inbox and send email after you confirm out loud, using a real Google sign-in — no passwords typed into this app.

One-time setup in Google's own console (about 5 minutes), then it's just the button below from now on:

  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com, sign in, and create a project (any name).
  2. Search for "Gmail API" and click Enable.
  3. Go to APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen, click Get started, choose External, and fill in an app name and your email.
  4. Still there, click the Audience tab → Test users → Add users, and type the exact Gmail address you plan to sign in with. Click Save.
  5. Click Clients → Create Client, choose Desktop app, then copy the Client ID and Client Secret it shows you.
⚠️ The step almost everyone misses is #4 — if you sign in below and see Google's "Access blocked… Error 403: access_denied" page, it means your email wasn't added as a test user yet. Go back to Audience → Test users, add it, then come back and try Sign in with Google again.

Because this stays a personal, unverified project, Google will also ask you to redo the sign-in below about once a week — that's a Google limit on personal apps, not a bug, and Voice AI will tell you plainly when it's time.
