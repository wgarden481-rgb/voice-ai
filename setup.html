Connect the AI and permissions

Voice AI uses a free open-source AI (OpenAI's gpt-oss) through Groq, which needs no credit card. Create a free account, make an API key at console.groq.com/keys, and paste it here.

Email contacts (optional)

Save names here so you can later say "email Mom" and the AI knows who you mean. Connecting Gmail itself (a quick Google sign-in) happens afterwards, in Settings.

How it works

Voice AI runs quietly in the background (look for its icon near the clock).

1. Hold Left Ctrl + Left Alt and ask a question, like "Who is the tallest man ever?"

2. Let go of the keys. Your question appears in a small box at the top-middle of the screen.

3. The AI speaks its answer out loud and writes it under your question.

Live info: ask about news, or "open YouTube", or "what does this say" about your screen.

Email: right-click the tray icon > Settings to sign in with Google (about 5 minutes, one time). After that, say "Email Mom that I'll be late" — it reads the draft back and only sends after you hold the keys again and say "yes".

You can change your language, voice, AI key, Gmail or contacts anytime from the Voice AI icon near your clock > Settings.
